Questions

Does the platform use ChatGPT, Gemini or another outside AI?

No. The evaluation engines are our own and run on our platform. In this prototype they use rule-based scoring; trained models come next. Your answers and projects are not sent to any outside AI service.

Is a human involved in my evaluation?

In this prototype the AI evaluates and issues the badge itself when it is confident, and a human check is optional: if the AI is not confident, you can retake the step or ask for an optional human review. Before any real launch, human approval will be required for every badge.

Do I pay to find my skill gap?

No. The basic assessment, skill analysis and feedback are free.

What if my score is low?

It is a starting point. You get your gaps, a learning path and a practical challenge, then you reassess.

Does Google or LinkedIn sign-in verify my skills?

No. Only AI assessment, project evaluation and a viva can verify a skill. We take only your name, email and photo.
